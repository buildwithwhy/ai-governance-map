What no binding rule reaches yet

Compute KYC, liability rules for harmful outputs, and international verification regimes are proposed somewhere — binding nowhere yet. Mandatory third-party auditing of frontier developers is enacted only in Illinois (from 2028), and statutory incident reporting for frontier models only in the EU, California, and (from 2027) New York and Illinois.
